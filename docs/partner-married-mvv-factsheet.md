# Factsheet: partner-married-mvv

Route file: `routes/partner-married-mvv.yaml`  
Last verified: 2026-11-18

## Summary

MVV + residence permit for the married or registered partner of a Dutch national or foreign resident in the Netherlands. Non-EU/EEA track only. Applicant applies from abroad via the Dutch embassy or consulate.

## Key numbers (verify before advising)

| Item | Value as of 2026-11-18 |
|------|------------------------|
| Fee | EUR 254 |
| Decision period | 90 calendar days |
| MVV validity after positive decision | 90 days |
| Minimum age (applicant + reference holder) | 21 years |

## Open uncertainties (section 7)

1. **Income threshold exact amount**: The IND partner page and the independent-income page do not show the same headline number. The 100% / 70% rule is stated, but the actual euro amount per month is on a separate page that may change with the minimum wage updates (1 January and 1 July). Always retrieve the current amount from [https://ind.nl/en/independent-sustainable-and-sufficient-income](https://ind.nl/en/independent-sustainable-and-sufficient-income) at the time of advice.

2. **Dutch partner page not machine-readable**: The Dutch-language partner page (`/nl/verblijfsvergunningen/familie-en-partner/verblijfsvergunning-voor-partner-aanvragen`) may contain additional or different requirements not reflected in the English page. If advising Dutch-speaking applicants, verify against the Dutch source.

3. **Civic integration exam exemptions**: The list of exempt nationalities is updated periodically. The route file lists the most common exempt countries, but the definitive list is at [https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/family-and-partner/residence-permit-for-partner](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/family-and-partner/residence-permit-for-partner) under the civic integration section.

4. **Antecedents certificate requirements**: Country-specific apostille or legalisation requirements are not modelled in the route YAML. A separate lookup per country of origin is needed.

5. **EU/EEA nationals and free movement**: EU/EEA nationals and their family members may have different rights under EU law (Directive 2004/38/EC). This route covers only the standard non-EU/EEA track. The IND page [verification-against-eu-law-for-union-citizen-family-members](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/family-and-partner/verification-against-eu-law-for-union-citizen-family-members) covers those cases.

6. **B07001E form availability**: The PDF form is referenced but its direct download URL was not captured. Confirm availability at the IND forms page.
